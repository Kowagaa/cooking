#Recipe
# Context

This is a recipe for Japanese curry that's good to get some veggies in, another good similar recipe would be my [[Japanese Milk Curry]], both of these require some [[Japanese Curry Roux]], which you can either buy or make your own


# Recipe
**Prep Time:** 5-6 min
**Cook Time:** 19-21 min
**Total:** 24-27 min
**Difficulty:**  (          ⭐⭐⭐/⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)
## Materials

- A pot / wok (I find a wok works best)
- A knife
- Something to stir the pot / wok with
## Ingredients

(1 Serving)
- [[Japanese Curry Roux]]
- Water
- 1 Medium Sized Onion
- 1 Medium Sized Potato
- 1 Small to Medium Sized Carrot
- Your preferred seed oil
- Cooked Rice (Preferrably [[Japanese Rice]])
# Prep

1) Dice your onion(s) into around 1cm diameter pieces
2) Peel and cut your potato(es) into around 1.5cm diameter pieces
3) Peel and cut your carrot(s) into slices and cut the bigger slices into halves or quarters if its a big carrot
# Method

1) Sauté the onion pieces until the edges start browning
2) Add potato and carrot pieces to the pot and stir until mixed
3) Add water until it almost covers all the ingredients and bring to a boil, remove scum as it rises
4) Add [[Japanese Curry Roux]] to taste (I recommend about enough to make the water almost opaque if not completely opaque)
5) Once the potato and carrot pieces can be squished slightly, it is ready and can be served over your rice
