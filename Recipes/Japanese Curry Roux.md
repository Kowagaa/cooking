# Context

This is my recipe for Japanese curry roux, though I only really recommend making it if you cant find a store that sells it, the store-bought version is usually better
# Recipe
**Prep Time:** 1min
**Cook Time:** 5 min
**Total:** 6 min
**Difficulty:** (             ⭐⭐/⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)
## Materials
- A pan
- A spatula
## Ingredients

- Flour
- Butter
- Curry
- Cayenne Powder
- Ginger
# Method

1) Melt butter into pan
2) Add flour and mix until you get a paste
3) Add 7 parts curry 2 parts ginger and one part cayenne powder until you get a nice dark brown paste
4) Put your paste into the fridge for at least 30 mins before use
