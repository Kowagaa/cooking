# Context
This recipe is for my favorite type of curry: Chicken Saag, though really most of this recipe is for the sauce itself, you can pour it over pretty much anything though


# Recipe
**Prep Time:** 8 min
**Cook Time:** 20-22 min
**Total:** 28-30 min
**Difficulty:** (        ⭐⭐⭐⭐/⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)

## Materials
- A Saucepan (Or preferably a wok)
- A Pan (Unnecessary if using a wok)
- Something to stir it all with
- A blender
- A separate bowl
## Ingredients
- Chicken, any part works as long as it is boneless (400g)
- Spinach (150g)
- Ground Coriander
- [[Garam Masala]]
- Ground Turmeric
- Ground Cumin
- Ground Cardamom
- Chili Powder (NOT FLAKES, POWDER)
- Tomato Paste (2 tsp or to taste)
- Yogurt (1 tbsp)
- Your choice of seed oil
- 2 Onion
# Prep
1) Dice your onions into 1cm diameter pieces
2) Cut your chicken into 1 inch / 2.5cm pieces
# Method
1) Sauté your onions until golden-golden brown in your saucepan / wok
2) Add herbs and spices to pan to taste (I recommend using a lot of [[Garam Masala]], it is what brings out the most flavor in this dish)
3) Add Tomato paste to water in separate bowl and stir till properly mixed, then add mixture to Sautéd onions (Note: It should just about cover the onions)
4) Add spinach to your mixture and make sure it is more or less covered by the orange mixture
5) Let it cook at the same temp as you sautéd the onions until the spinach is wilted
6) Remove from heat and blend until you have something like on the right of this picture ![[chicken saag 0.jpg]]
7) Cook the chicken in a bit of oil in your pan / wok until golden
8) Pour the spinach over the chicken and add you tbsp of yogurt, then mix and voila, you have chicken saag
