# Context

This is a recipe on how to make rice for sushi and other Japanese dishes

# Recipe
**Prep Time:** 2 mins
**Cook Time:** 28 mins
**Total:** 30 mins
**Difficulty:** (                 ⭐/⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)
## Materials
- A strainer
- A Rice cooker
## Ingredients
(Per serving of rice)
- 1/10th cup of your preferred seed oil 
- 1/10th cup rice vinegar
- Half a tsp. salt
- Half a tsp. sugar
- Rice
- Water
# Prep

1) Wash your rice

# Method

1) Put your rice along with the necessary water in the rice cooker and let it cook
2) Once its ready add the rice vinegar, oil, salt & sugar to the rice and mix it
3) Close the rice cooker and let it cool down
4) De-clump the rice once its cooled down and serve
